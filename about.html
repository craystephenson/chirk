About me

Hi there. Here’s a bit about me.

I am a bit of a hybrid creative as I have tons of general market experience but am also an aspiring technologist.

I also love A/V. I’m a songwriter (working with my daughter) and an okay producer. I’m also very experienced with editorial. Pro Tools and Premiere are my tools of choice.
